Frequently asked questions

Where does an article open?

Each article card links to its own file under /en/articles/. For example: /en/articles/article-name.html.

Why static HTML?

Many AI crawlers do not run JavaScript. Static HTML puts the important content in the initial source, which suits SEO, AEO and GEO.
