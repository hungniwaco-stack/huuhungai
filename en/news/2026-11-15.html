Anthropic introduces Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2

Anthropic starts the roadshow for its planned IPO

On Nov 10, Anthropic announced the start of the roadshow to offer Class A common stock, which it has registered to list on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT. The final price and share count are only set after the roadshow, so figures in the press are not yet final results.

Source: Anthropic

Nvidia announces a CEO change, with Colette Kress as interim CEO

On Nov 12, Nvidia said Jensen Huang resigned as chairman, CEO and board member, effective immediately. CFO Colette Kress serves as interim CEO, Harvey C. Jones is the first board chair, and the board has begun a search for a permanent CEO from inside and outside the company.

Source: Nvidia Investor Relations
