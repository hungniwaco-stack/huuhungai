## What this website collects
huuhungai.com is a reading blog. It has no accounts, no sign-up forms and no sales. The site uses **Google Tag Manager** and **Google Analytics 4** to measure traffic. These Google tools may record: the pages you view, how long you view them, the page that referred you, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city or country level). Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers to tell visits apart.
## Data stored in your browser
The light or dark theme button, the text size buttons and the language switcher save your choice in your browser (local storage) and send it nowhere. You can remove it by clearing site data in your browser.
## Third-party services
Measurement data is processed by Google under Google's policies. The site is hosted on Vercel. Articles link to outside sources; when you click through, the destination site has its own policy.
## Cookie consent box
On your first visit, a small box at the bottom of the screen asks whether you agree to Google Analytics measurement cookies. By default, until you agree, the site sets storage to denied for measurement and advertising (Google Consent Mode). While denied, Google tags may still send some signals that use no cookies and are not personally identifying; we do not confirm the details of those signals and you should read Google's Consent Mode documentation. Your choice is stored in your browser (key `hh-consent`), and you can change it at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.
## How you can control it
- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
- Use a private window or a tracker-blocking extension.
- Install Google's official add-on to opt out of Google Analytics: [tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

This page states what we know about current data collection and is not legal advice. If the policy changes, we will update this page and the update date at the top.
